# Comet

Comet is a Palm sized desk robot with a Space theme. Its big expressive eyes are drawn on a small OLED screen, and it can blink, look around, get sleepy, and light up when you pet it. A small servo lets it nod and tilt its head, and a touch sensor lets people interact with it at Maker Faire. I’m building it as a beginner, starting with the eyes and the nod, then adding extras like constellation animations, ears, and a little astronaut outfit if time allows.

As a teenager hardware stuff the stuff you can interct with always felt intresting and made be curious but starting it wasn't easy at all  somettins I was scared as hell that what if I fail or something breaks or how will I arrange money to buy parts, but hackclu helped me to give it a start, like  I am doing harward for the first time and it is hard as hell every part like wiring diagram, cad, pcb everything. I wanted to build something that felt alive, not just blinked an LED. I have always been a space enthu so what would could have been better that a robo with space theme, but I’d never designed an enclosure before, so Comet became my way of learning CAD properly: every wall thickness, every screw boss and every cable path had to actually work.
